# Inspect and visualize declarations

This part covers **GraphDefinition and GraphRegistry**. Inspect their metadata in code, print their textual reports, or browse their interactive diagrams. None of these operations executes a graph.

Start with one graph's fields and report, then its diagram. A registry lets you browse several prepared declarations and inspect shared resources or requirement conflicts.

```{toctree}
:maxdepth: 1

inspection
graphs
registry
```

Once you submit a graph, its outcome belongs to the returned ContextRun. Continue with [execution results and reports](execution.md) to inspect that separate evidence.
